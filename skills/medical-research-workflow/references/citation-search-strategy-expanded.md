# Citation Search Strategy Expanded

Use this module when building citations for Introduction, Discussion, guidelines, or clinical claims.

## Database Priority

Search in this order when available:

1. PubMed/MEDLINE for biomedical literature.
2. Cochrane Library for systematic reviews.
3. ClinicalTrials.gov or WHO ICTRP for trial registration.
4. Official guideline/regulatory sources: WHO, CDC, FDA, EMA, NICE, professional societies.
5. Crossref/publisher pages for DOI metadata.
6. Semantic Scholar/Google Scholar only as secondary discovery tools; verify final citations elsewhere.

## Evidence Priority

Prefer:

- Current clinical guidelines for recommendations.
- Systematic reviews/meta-analyses for evidence summaries.
- RCTs for intervention efficacy.
- High-quality cohort studies for prognosis/risk.
- Diagnostic accuracy studies for index tests.
- Original landmark papers when historically necessary.

## Search Construction

Use combinations of:

- Disease/condition terms.
- Population terms.
- Intervention/exposure/index test.
- Outcome terms.
- Study design filters.
- MeSH terms when available.
- "systematic review", "meta-analysis", "randomized", "cohort", "diagnostic accuracy", "guideline".

## Verification Rules

- Record PMID, PMCID, DOI, trial registration ID, or official URL.
- Verify that the source supports the exact claim.
- Check publication year and whether guidelines are superseded.
- Label preprints, editorials, letters, and conference abstracts.
- Do not use journal quartile as proof of truth; use it only as a rough source-quality heuristic.

## Manuscript Placement

- Introduction: burden, gap, rationale.
- Methods: reporting guideline, measurement instruments, validated definitions.
- Discussion: comparison with prior findings and plausible mechanisms.
- Limitations: cite known measurement or design limitations when needed.

## Reviewer Questions

- Are key claims supported by current and directly relevant evidence?
- Are citations primary evidence, evidence synthesis, guidelines, or opinion?
- Does each citation actually say what the manuscript claims?
- Are Q1/Q2 preferences used without excluding essential lower-tier or official sources?
