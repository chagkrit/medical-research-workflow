# Manuscript And Reporting

## Manuscript Structure

Use IMRaD unless the target journal requires another format.

- Title: identify design, population, exposure/intervention, and outcome when possible.
- Abstract: structured and consistent with main text.
- Introduction: clinical/scientific gap, rationale, objective/hypothesis.
- Methods: design, setting, participants, variables, data sources, bias, sample size, statistics, ethics.
- Results: flow, baseline characteristics, primary results, secondary results, sensitivity analyses, harms/adverse events when relevant.
- Discussion: principal findings, comparison with literature, mechanisms, strengths/limitations, implications, conclusion.

## Reporting Guideline Alignment

Select guideline by design and verify the current checklist from the official source:

- CONSORT: randomized trials.
- STROBE: observational cohort, case-control, cross-sectional studies.
- PRISMA: systematic reviews/meta-analyses.
- TRIPOD or TRIPOD-AI: prediction models.
- STARD: diagnostic accuracy.
- CARE: case reports.
- ARRIVE: animal studies.
- CHEERS: health economic evaluations.
- RECORD: routinely collected health data.

## Writing Rules

- Keep Methods reproducible.
- Keep Results free of interpretation.
- Avoid causal wording for non-causal designs.
- Avoid overstating subgroup, secondary, or exploratory findings.
- State absolute numbers with percentages.
- Use consistent denominators.
- Match every table/figure to a manuscript claim.

## Reviewer Questions

- Is the research gap real and supported by verified literature?
- Can the study be reproduced from Methods?
- Are results reported for all prespecified outcomes?
- Are limitations specific rather than generic?
- Does the conclusion stay within the evidence?
