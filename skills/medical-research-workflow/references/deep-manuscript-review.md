# Deep Manuscript Review

Use this module for simulated senior peer review before submission or major revision.

## Review Domains

1. Research gap clarity.
2. Novelty and contribution.
3. Research design aligned with research question.
4. Argument flow.
5. No overclaiming.
6. Claims supported by verified citations.
7. Structure aligned with objective.
8. Editor first-page test.
9. Reviewer anticipation.
10. Limitations acknowledged.
11. Generalizability.
12. Clinical or scientific significance.

## Output Format

For each domain report:

- Finding.
- Severity: critical, major, minor, acceptable.
- Likely reviewer/editor wording.
- Evidence from manuscript.
- Recommended revision.
- Whether the revision requires new analysis, new citation, rewriting, or scope reduction.

## Red Flags

- Objective does not match design.
- Results do not answer the stated primary question.
- Discussion claims causality from observational association.
- Limitations are generic.
- Novelty is asserted but not compared against verified literature.
- Abstract conclusion is stronger than Results.
- Manuscript hides missing data, exclusions, or failed assumptions.
- Tables/figures contradict text.

## Language Calibration

- Use "associated with" for observational associations unless causal assumptions are justified.
- Use "suggests" or "is consistent with" for exploratory findings.
- Use "may" for mechanistic interpretation not directly tested.
- Avoid "proves", "confirms", "definitive", "no difference" when uncertainty remains.
