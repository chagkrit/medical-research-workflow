# Manuscript Consistency And Figure QC

Use this module before submission, after table/figure edits, and after manuscript revisions.

## Text-To-Table/Figure Consistency

Check:

- Every table/figure cited in text exists.
- Every table/figure appears in citation order.
- Numbers in text match table/figure values.
- Direction of effect is consistent across abstract, results, discussion, and tables.
- Adjusted vs unadjusted estimates are not mixed.
- Denominators and missing data counts are consistent.
- Confidence intervals and p values match output.
- Subgroup and sensitivity findings are labeled correctly.

## Caption And Legend QC

Captions must state:

- Population/subset analyzed.
- Outcome or measure.
- Group definitions.
- Time point/follow-up when relevant.
- Model adjustment or weighting when relevant.
- Abbreviations.

## Figure Quality

Check:

- Axis labels include units.
- Text is readable at journal column size.
- Labels do not overlap.
- Colors are distinguishable in grayscale or colorblind-safe palettes.
- Error bars and CIs are defined.
- KM curves include number-at-risk table when required.
- ROC figures report AUC with CI.
- Forest plots align labels, estimates, and CIs.

## Reviewer Questions

- Can a reviewer reproduce each textual claim from a table/figure?
- Are any results selectively emphasized?
- Are captions sufficient without reading the full Methods?
- Would the figure remain readable after journal resizing?
