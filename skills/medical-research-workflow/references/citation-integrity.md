# Citation Integrity

## Approved Source Types

Prefer traceable sources:

- PubMed/MEDLINE for biomedical journal articles.
- DOI/Crossref and publisher pages for bibliographic metadata.
- Cochrane Library for systematic reviews.
- ClinicalTrials.gov, EU Clinical Trials Register, or WHO ICTRP for trials.
- FDA, EMA, WHO, CDC, NIH, NICE, professional society guideline pages, or journal-hosted guidelines for official recommendations.
- EQUATOR Network or official guideline pages for reporting checklists.

## Verification Workflow

For each citation:

1. Search authoritative database.
2. Confirm title, authors, journal/source, year, DOI/PMID/PMCID/registration ID.
3. Confirm the cited claim is actually supported by the source.
4. Prefer primary studies for study-specific evidence and guidelines/systematic reviews for summary recommendations.
5. Store source URL and access date for web documents.

Read `references/citation-search-strategy-expanded.md` when building a citation set for Introduction, Discussion, guidelines, or evidence synthesis. Use it for database order, evidence hierarchy, search construction, and reviewer-facing citation checks.

## Anti-Hallucination Rules

- Do not create plausible-looking references.
- Do not infer DOI/PMID from memory.
- Do not cite an abstract as if full text was reviewed.
- Do not cite retracted, superseded, or preprint evidence without labeling it.
- Do not use non-authoritative websites for clinical claims when a guideline, regulator, or indexed article is available.

## Reviewer Questions

- Does each key claim have a source?
- Are the most important sources current enough for the topic?
- Are guidelines superseded?
- Are citations primary evidence, review evidence, or opinion pieces?
- Is any citation being used beyond what it actually supports?
